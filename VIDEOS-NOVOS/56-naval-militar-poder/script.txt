They carry more firepower than most countries. Aircraft carriers are the ultimate symbol of naval dominance, and only a handful of nations can build them.

The United States operates eleven nuclear-powered supercarriers, more than every other nation combined. The USS Gerald R. Ford, commissioned in 2017, is the most advanced warship ever built, displacing over one hundred thousand tons and carrying more than seventy-five aircraft.

China has rapidly expanded its fleet. The Fujian, launched in 2022, is its first carrier with electromagnetic catapults, a technology previously exclusive to the United States.

The United Kingdom fields two Queen Elizabeth-class carriers, each displacing sixty-five thousand tons. France operates the Charles de Gaulle, the only nuclear-powered carrier outside the American fleet.

Russia's Admiral Kuznetsov remains in extended repair, while India commissioned the INS Vikrant in 2022, making it one of only six nations to build a carrier domestically.

The ocean belongs to those who control its skies.
