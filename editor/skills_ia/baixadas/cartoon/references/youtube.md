# YouTube upload checklist (checked against YouTube Help, October 2026)

## 1 · Audience: "Made for kids"
If the video is made for children, you must say so. In YouTube Studio: **Create → Upload → Basic info → Audience → "Yes, it's made for kids"** (for an existing video: Content → tick it → Edit → Audience).
What that turns off on the video (YouTube Help, "Features unavailable on made for kids content"): comments, personalized ads, notifications, end screens and cards, the Miniplayer, Save to playlist / Watch Later, channel memberships, Super Chat and live chat, merch. Videos set as made for kids are more likely to be recommended next to other kids' videos.
Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097 and /answer/9527654

## 2 · The AI label
YouTube asks you to disclose AI content that **looks realistic** (real people, real events, realistic scenes). A clearly animated cartoon doesn't need the label: "Creators don't need to disclose non-realistic content that's made with AI." If you add realistic elements, use the "AI use" setting during upload.
Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

## 3 · Monetization: the "inauthentic content" rule
Since July 15, 2025, YouTube's "repetitious content" rule is called **inauthentic content**. These don't earn money: template-made videos with little variation, "templated storylines", and videos that put characters in the same situation with the same outcome again and again. These are fine: a shared intro and outro, and a series where each video has its own storyline. Using AI is fine when the result shows your own creative vision (your characters, your stories).
So, for every video:
- A new story, a new problem, a new ending. Don't recycle one plot with new names.
- Your own characters and your own world (series bible), so the channel looks like yours.
- A shared intro and "The End" card are fine.
Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

## 4 · Chapters
Put timestamps in the description: the first one at **00:00**, at least **3**, in order, each chapter **10 seconds or longer**. Use the story titles.
Source: support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579

## 5 · Title, thumbnail, description
- **Title:** what it is + length + mood: "1 Hour of Calm Bedtime Stories for Kids | Felt Animation".
- **Thumbnail:** one character big, one warm light, 2–4 words max. Render it from a scene (`npx hyperframes snapshot --at <time>`) and add the words.
- **Description:** 2 lines about the stories, then the chapters.

## 6 · Before you publish
- Watch the whole video once at full speed, with sound.
- Check the loudness (around −16 LUFS) and that no scene is silent or black.
- Upload at 1080p or higher.
