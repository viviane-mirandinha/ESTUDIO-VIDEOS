# Prickles (warm, ~31 s story)

**Look:** coloured pencil and watercolour on cream paper, rust, mustard, sage and cream, golden-hour light that ends at dusk with fireflies. Picture-book animals with natural proportions. Only the part that acts moves; each face changes once and holds.
**Voice:** Katherine – Calm Luxury Narrator (Eleven v4).
**Sound:** soft felt piano, glockenspiel and pizzicato; a lonely solo piano at "curled up small", a warm swell at the hug; a comic "boing" on OUCH!, leaf rustles, a chime for every leaf.

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs)
[warmly] [softly] Deep in the autumn forest… lived a little hedgehog named Prickles. [pause] Prickles loved hugs more than anything. [curious] But every time she tried… [surprised] OUCH! [sighs] So Prickles curled up small… and hid in the leaves. [pause] [gently hopeful] Then her friends had an idea. [excited] They gathered the softest leaves in the forest… [delighted] and her prickles held every single one. [warmly] And then… the biggest hug of all. [softly] [slowly] Because the right hug… always finds you.
