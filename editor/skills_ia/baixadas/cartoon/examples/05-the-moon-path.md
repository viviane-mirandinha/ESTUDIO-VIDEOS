# The Moon Path (calm bedtime, ~29 s story)

**Look:** a paper diorama beach at night: layered paper dunes, felt sea-grass, a full moon low over the sea and a shimmering "moon path" of foil-paper strips on the waves, stars on threads, a boardwalk with three warm amber lamps (the "wrong light").
**Characters:** Pip, a coin-sized baby sea turtle (sage felt shell with stitched hexagons, big bead eye); Ghost Crab (cream felt, eyes on stalks); the Fireflies (glowing beads on fine wire); Gull.
**Voice:** Katherine – Calm Luxury Narrator (Eleven v4), e.g. `[softly, warm, unhurried]`, `[a little worried]` in scene 5.
**Sound:** a music-box lullaby that slips into a minor key when the lamps switch on and comes back to major when the friends arrive; soft surf, crickets, an eggshell crack, three lamp clicks, a firefly chime per light, a splash.

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs)
[softly, warm, unhurried] On a quiet beach, under a silver moon… a tiny turtle named Pip hatched from her shell. [pause] Every turtle knows the way: follow the moon to the sea. [pause] But the boardwalk lamps glowed brighter… and Pip turned the wrong way. [a little worried] She was lost… and far too small to see the sea. [long pause] [gently hopeful] Then her friends came… each showing her the way. [warm, smiling] And Pip followed the moon… all the way home.
