# The Lighthouse Mouse (calm bedtime, ~29 s story) · 61,000+ views on Instagram

**Look:** felt characters (bead eyes, running stitches) in a paper diorama at night: dark navy sea in layered paper waves, a lighthouse beam, stars hanging on threads. Characters move on twos (a stop-motion feel); the camera, water and light stay smooth.
**Voice:** Katherine – Calm Luxury Narrator (Eleven v4).
**Sound:** a music-box lullaby, sea and rain, a thunder hit, a soft "whump" when the light comes back.

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs)
[slowly, softly, a warm bedtime storyteller reading to a sleepy child] At the edge of the sea… stood a lighthouse. [pause] And a tiny mouse named Juniper… kept it shining. [long pause] So every boat could find its way home. [long pause] [hushed] But one stormy night… [pause] the light went out. [long pause] [tender, a little sad] She was far too small… to light it alone. [long pause] [gently hopeful] Then her friends came… [pause] each with a little light. [long pause] [warm, smiling] And together… they were bright enough.
