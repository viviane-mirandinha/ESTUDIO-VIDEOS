# The Paper Rocket (bright, ~34 s story) · built 100% in code: this is the film in kit/

**Look:** flat felt and paper shapes with fibre texture and stitched edges, daytime colours, story words popping in inside each scene, scallop paper wipes, a new scene every 1.5–3 s. Upbeat marimba groove (kit/audio/groove.py).
**Voice:** a bright, playful storyteller (Eleven v4).

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs)
[bright, playful, excited storyteller, lively pace] Once upon a time… a little fox named Pip dreamed of touching the moon. So Pip built a rocket — out of boxes, tape and paint! [building suspense] Three… two… one… [shouting with joy] BLAST OFF! [fast, thrilled] Up past the clouds, through a flock of birds, over a rainbow… and into the stars! [delighted] Pip waved at Saturn… and landed — [playful] bump! — on the moon. [warm, proud] Dreams are made to fly.
