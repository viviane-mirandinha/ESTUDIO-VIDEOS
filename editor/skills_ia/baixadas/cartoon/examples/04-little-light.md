# Little Light (calm bedtime, ~29 s story)

**Look:** felt and paper at night, deep under the sea: a moonlit surface seen from below, rippling paper light beams, dark layered paper rock arches and thread kelp. Everything in the deep has a light inside it, switched off until the reveal.
**Characters:** Bo, a baby blue whale in slate-blue felt with a stitched cream belly; a tiny dark-felt lanternfish with one glowing bead; jellyfish (vellum bells, thread tentacles), glowing plankton, a paper coral garden; her family as big felt silhouettes.
**Voice:** Katherine – Calm Luxury Narrator (Eleven v4), e.g. `[softly, warm, unhurried]`, `[whispering]` for "Follow me", `[a little wonder]` at the reveal.
**Sound:** a music-box lullaby, sparse and low in the dark, then a celesta bloom at the reveal; a muffled underwater hum, bubbles, distant whale calls, a soft "blink" ping on each light.

## Narration (paste into ElevenLabs)
[softly, warm, unhurried] Deep in the sea, where the moonlight never reaches… lived a little whale named Bo, who was scared of the dark. [pause] Every night, her family dove down deep… but Bo stayed behind. [long pause] Then, down in the dark… something blinked. [pause] [whispering] "Follow me," it whispered. [pause] [a little wonder] And one by one… the whole sea lit up. [warm, smiling] The dark was never empty. It was full of little lights.
